merchant
/datum/computer_file/program/merchant

Vars

_hub_refA weakref to the currently connected trade hub, if any.
_merchant_refA weakref to the merchant that is currently being interacted with, if any.
_pad_refA weakref to the merchant pad next to the console, if any.
last_commsHolds a string containing the most recent 'message' sent to the player from the merchant.
prompt_messageIf not null, the UI window will show the message inside this var, until the player clicks the 'Continue' button.
stored_cashHolds cash used to trade with merchants, in the format of /decl/currency/foo = 5000.

Procs

adjust_cashChanges how much of a specific currency the computer contains, removing it entirely if it runs out.
ask_appraisalAsks the merchant to say how much they would pay for everything on the merchant pad, assuming they want it.

ask_priceCalled when the user clicks on the button to ask for an item's price on the UI.
ask_what_they_wantAsks the merchant what items they want to buy.

attempt_sellAttempts to sell everything on the merchant pad, in exchange for money.
bribe_merchantCalled when the user clicks one of the bribe buttons in the UI.
check_hubChecks whether the inputted trade hub is valid to trade with.
check_items_on_padChecks that everything on top of the pad are things the merchant won't object to receiving.
check_merchant_disconnectionReturns TRUE if the merchant is fed up and cuts the connection.
compliment_merchantCalled when the user clicks the compliment button in the UI.
connect_to_hubConnects to the targeted hub.
delete_items_on_padDeletes all valid objects on top of the merchant pad. Called when the user successfully sell to or barters with a merchant.
deposit_cashPlaces cash objects that are on top of the pad into the stored_cash list.
get_cashReturns how much of a specific currency the computer currently contains.
haggle_buyAttempts to buy a specific item from the merchant, at a price defined by the user, using money.
haggle_sellAttempts to sell everything on the merchant pad, at a price defined by the user, in exchange for money.
hail_merchantAttempts to hail the target merchant.
handle_no_common_languageCalled when the merchant receives a question from the user, when the user doesn't speak any of their languages.
insult_merchantCalled when the user clicks the insult button in the UI, or rarely if they don't speak any of the merchant's languages.
print_price_modifiersReturns a string describing all of the price modifiers in the inputted list. You probably don't want to show [supply|demand]_price_modifiers to players, since it might be weird to be told that a merchant is charging XX% more because the player had low finance skill.
scroll_merchant_selectionCalled when the user clicks one of the two arrows in the UI to 'scroll' to different available merchants.
withdraw_cashRemoves the cash held inside of stored_cash back onto the pad.
