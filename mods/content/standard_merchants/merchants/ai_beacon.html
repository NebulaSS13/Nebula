/datum/merchant/trading_beaconA trading outpost managed by a fairly sophisticated artificial intelligence. They ask for a wide range of random things, and sell products within their particular focus. Like other AI-themed merchants, they are highly skilled in trade and are likely to have an advantage in negotiations. They will also not play favorites, and will not consider disposition as a factor in pricing. They also essentially have infinite patience and won't care about most things that might offend merchants made out of flesh.
