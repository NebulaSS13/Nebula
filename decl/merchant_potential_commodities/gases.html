gases
/decl/merchant_potential_commodities/gases

Variant which creates gas commodities. Only works with demand.

Vars

container_typesWhat the gas should be inside of. Due to return_air() reasons, only /obj/item/tank and /obj/machinery/portable_atmospherics is allowed.
gas_mixesNested list containing gas mixes and their ratios to add as commodities. Each mix is contained inside of an inner assoc list, with the material path as the key, and the ratio as the value.
target_temperatureHow hot each gas should be in order to be accepted. If blank, no temperature requirement will be added.
