rotate_over_time
/decl/merchant_inventory_strategy/rotate_over_time

Shifts a number of items between the two lists every so often. If the merchant is allowed to exist for long enough, they will eventually complete a full cycle and bring back things they 'put away', and vice versa.

Vars

initial_percentagePercentage of total items which are moved between lists every rotation.
rotated_percentageHow often should things be rotated, in minutes.

Procs

setup_inventoryPercentage of total items which are initially available. If rotated_percentage is lower than this, some items will remain available every time the inventory rotates.
